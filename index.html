About Meghavi Comferts

Meghavi Comferts was founded in 1997 with a vision to create extraordinary celebrations and turn wedding dreams into unforgettable experiences.

With a strong association with the Taj Group, the brand has built its expertise around refined hospitality, thoughtful planning, and exceptional event experiences.

Led by Pankaj Kapoor, Meghavi Comferts brings together experience, creativity, design, and meticulous execution to craft celebrations that feel personal, elegant, and truly memorable.

From intimate gatherings to grand weddings, every celebration is thoughtfully designed to transform your vision into an experience worth remembering.

OUR PHILOSOPHY

At Meghavi Comferts, every celebration begins with a vision — and evolves into an experience that feels truly extraordinary.

Built on a legacy of experience, creativity, and refined execution, we look beyond conventional wedding planning to create celebrations that reflect the individuality of every couple. Our approach brings together innovative concepts, thoughtful design, cultural richness, and contemporary aesthetics to create moments that are both timeless and distinctive.

From intimate celebrations to grand destination weddings, every detail is thoughtfully curated — from the first idea to the final execution. Meghavi Comferts is where imagination meets experience, and every wedding becomes a story of its own.

Our Services

Meghavi Comferts is here to orchestrate your perfect celebration.

We'll work with you to create a bespoke experience that harmonizes every element of your wedding: Creative Design, Guest Hospitality, Global Logistics, Entertainment, and Legacy Planning.

Wedding Planning & Project Management

A world-class wedding is made up of countless details, decisions, and moving parts. At Meghavi Comferts, we take care of the journey from concept to completion, so you can be fully present for your celebration.

From the initial consultation and venue visits to planning, coordination, vendor management, and final execution, our team manages every detail with precision and care.

We create a clear, structured roadmap around your vision and ensure every element comes together seamlessly — allowing you to experience your wedding as a celebration, not a project to manage.

You dream it. We plan it. We bring it to life.

Design & Decor

We don't just decorate spaces. We build worlds.

If Project Management is the heartbeat of a wedding, Design is its soul. At Meghavi Comferts, we believe your celebration should be a living, breathing reflection of your personality. We don't trends; we set them. Our design philosophy is about creating "theatrical intimacy" - grand enough to take your breath away, yet personal enough to feel like home.

From the first mood board to the final floral petal, we weave a visual narrative that stays with your guests long after the lights go down.

Entertainment & Experiences

At Meghavi Comferts, entertainment is more than a performance — it is an integral part of the celebration. We curate exceptional artists, performers, and entertainers to create moments that energise the atmosphere and stay with your guests long after the celebration ends.

From live bands, renowned DJs, and classical ensembles to cultural performances, dancers, aerial artists, celebrity acts, and immersive experiences, every element is thoughtfully selected to complement the scale, style, and spirit of your celebration.

We curate the talent. You create the memories.
